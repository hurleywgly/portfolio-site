what’s varying

All three keep your structure — headline grab, a few sentences, contact and socials in reach — and all three carry the proof ethos (“runs daily” / “I use it”) and name you as a product person. What varies is the opening move: A leads with the proof claim, B leads with warmth and lets the identity line do the work, C leads with the pattern behind everything you’ve built.

Pieces are mixable — e.g. B’s identity line under A’s headline, or C’s pattern paragraph as the second paragraph of either.

open fact rulings
