Selection thesis

Clear at a glance.

The winners reduce each skill to one legible action: extract, inspect, challenge, compress, package, scan. Their silhouettes survive at card size and their gold accents perform a job.

The collection is strongest when it feels like a coherent world of capable little machines—not a set of ornate diagrams.
