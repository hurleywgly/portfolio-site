03 · The Five Rules (v3)

The argument, numbered.

1 · emphasis strategy

Light inverts, dark restrains.

Light: forest cards on pale sage — contrast carries hierarchy. Dark: one slate family — gold + position carry hierarchy. Never powder-invert dark.

2 · accent dosage

Gold in small doses only.

NEW badges, active nav, selected-row rail, kickers, one link per cluster. Never large fills. Gold is accent only.

3 · lattice grammar

Uneven marks in open space.

Thin rules + L-brackets + hollow squares + filled junction dots. Never hollow circles, never even grids, nothing within 24px of a card edge or text block.

4 · nav

Labeled everywhere, lowercase mono.

Real hand-drawn glyphs beside every word. One shared glass header on every route; active page underlined gold. 'writing' is the external blog.

5 · chrome

Kickers gold, labels tracked, home just ends.

Kickers are // lowercase mono in accent. Section labels are UPPER TRACKED MONO. Footers carry the socials row (About: BASED · SEATTLE, WA). The ● GRID coordinate tag is retired. Home is footer-less by design.

06 · Implementation Canon

How it ships.

chromeOne shared glass header on every route — nav geometry byte-identical everywhere. Pages crop their frame's baked chrome band (DESK_TOP). Mobile bottom bar is md:hidden.
marginsDesktop text sits on the exhibit-shell line (1280 max + 80 pad). Stage pages place content at 160..1280 stage coords. The frames' 80..1360 box is superseded.
mobile modelMock scale: the 1054 artboard scaled as one object, landing un-zoomed. Projects + Tools are the two reflow pages, tracking the artboard via vw. Reflowed-native-mobile is rejected canon.
projects flowDesktop hover previews, click launches. Mobile lands unselected: tap → preview → tap again → visit. Selection is pointer-type guarded.
art exportsReal Figma nodes only — never approximations, never page-region crops. Shelf = transparent composed SVG per theme; check every re-export for <image> tags and artboard rects.
metadataTitle stays "AI Systems Builder" (the "& product manager" lives in the og-card kicker only). Self-canonicals; JSON-LD Person/WebSite/ProfilePage; pages defining openGraph must spread OG_IMAGE; llms.txt mirrors the projects copy.
skill copyNotion page "Ryan's Skills" is the source of truth for /tools playbook copy — sync, never author in code.
